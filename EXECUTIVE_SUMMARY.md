# Executive Summary — Amman Digital Market Analytics

## Top Findings

1. Monthly revenue shows clear peaks, with the highest in June 2025 (5,086.5 JOD) and March 2025 (4,309.5 JOD).
2. Amman generates the most revenue (15,719 JOD), followed by Irbid (7,250.5 JOD) and Zarqa (4,842 JOD).
3. The top 2 product categories by revenue are Books (12,991 JOD) and Electronics (12,937 JOD).

## Recommendations

1. **Prepare Inventory for Peak Months:** Ensure stock is sufficient for March and June based on revenue trends.
2. **Focus Marketing on High-Revenue Cities:** Target Amman and Irbid for campaigns while exploring growth in smaller cities.
3. **Promote High-Revenue Categories:** Increase promotion and offers for Books and Electronics to maximize revenue.
