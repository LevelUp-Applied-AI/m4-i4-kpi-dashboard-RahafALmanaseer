# KPI Framework — Amman Digital Market

Define 5 KPIs for the Amman Digital Market. At least 2 must be time-based and 1 must be cohort-based.

---

## KPI 1
- **Name:** Total Revenue
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated from all orders (excluding cancelled orders and suspicious quantities)
- **Formula:** SUM(quantity * unit_price)
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, orders.status
- **Baseline Value:** 56,261.5 JOD
- **Interpretation:** Shows overall business performance and market size.

---

## KPI 2
- **Name:** Average Order Value
- **Definition:** Average revenue per order
- **Formula:** SUM(quantity * unit_price) / COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, orders.order_id
- **Baseline Value:** 112.52 JOD
- **Interpretation:** Indicates customer spending behavior per order.

---

## KPI 3
- **Name:** Monthly Revenue
- **Definition:** Total revenue aggregated per month
- **Formula:** SUM(quantity * unit_price) grouped by MONTH(order_date)
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, orders.order_date
- **Baseline Value:** See chart (ranges from 2,021 JOD to 5,086.5 JOD)
- **Interpretation:** Reveals trends, seasonality, and peak months.

---

## KPI 4
- **Name:** Revenue by City
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated by customers in each city
- **Formula:** SUM(quantity * unit_price) grouped by city
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, customers.city, orders.customer_id
- **Baseline Value:** Amman: 15,719 JOD, Irbid: 7,250.5 JOD, others see chart
- **Interpretation:** Highlights which cities contribute most to revenue (cohort-based KPI).

---

## KPI 5
- **Name:** Top 5 Product Categories by Revenue
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated by each product category, top 5
- **Formula:** SUM(quantity * unit_price) grouped by category, sorted descending
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, products.category
- **Baseline Value:** Books: 12,991 JOD, Electronics: 12,937 JOD, Clothing: 11,461 JOD, Home & Garden: 8,590 JOD, Food & Beverage: 5,272.5 JOD
- **Interpretation:** Identifies best-performing categories to focus marketing and stock decisions.
